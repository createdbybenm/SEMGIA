# M02 rationale

I support our choice to test the model on a whole new session (E06) and to pick the model with leave-one-session-out,
instead of mixing random windows from all recordings. Windows from the same placement share the same electrode gain
and skin contact, so a random split would let the model learn the placement and not the contraction, and the score
would look better than it really is. Young et al. showed that moving the electrodes by only 1 cm clearly raised the
error of EMG pattern recognition, so a new placement is the real test. In live use every session starts with new
electrodes, and that is what our split measures.

Source: Young AJ, Hargrove LJ, Kuiken TA. The effects of electrode size and orientation on the sensitivity of
myoelectric pattern recognition systems to electrode shift. IEEE Trans Biomed Eng 2011;58(9):2537-44.
doi:10.1109/TBME.2011.2159216
