# M03 rationale

I doubt our choice to record with the DFRobot SEN0240 and its dry electrodes built into the board. I connected
the sensor to the ESP32 (GPIO 34), worked on the firmware and recorded the sessions, and I saw the problem
myself: dry electrodes lose contact during a session, the 60 Hz hum grows, and the signal hits the limit of the
converter. 17 of our 59 recordings were rejected for this reason. The sensor also has no analog 60 Hz notch, so
we had to add a digital one. For the next sessions I would use gel electrodes, a tight strap and the laptop on
battery, and check that the signal is quiet at rest before each recording.

Source: DFRobot. Gravity: Analog EMG Sensor by OYMotion (SKU SEN0240), product wiki.
