# M04 rationale

I support our choice to keep every recording in Microsoft Azure, in a European region (belgiumcentral), in a
private storage account and with codes instead of names (P002, OP01). Muscle signals are health data, and in
Mexico the LFPDPPP treats health data as sensitive personal data, so we must keep it safe and as small as
possible. With codes, nobody can link a recording to a person without the paper list. Our student account only
allowed a few regions, and belgiumcentral was the only one in Europe. Azure also lets us delete one recording
everywhere with one command if the person asks.

Source: Microsoft. Data residency in Azure.
https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/explore/global-infrastructure/data-residency/
