# M01 rationale

I support using timer-based labels to identify rest and contraction periods, with 8 seconds assigned to each phase.
This makes the labeling consistent across all recordings because the labels depend on the same timing rule rather than
someone judging the EMG signal by eye. However, one possible weakness is human reaction time. During recording, I
noticed that the webpage updates approximately every 150 ms, so the visual cue might appear slightly after the label
changes. Xiao et al. reported visual reaction times of approximately 0.33 seconds, increasing to 0.39 seconds after
fatigue in their study. Since we skip 1.5 seconds around each transition, I believe this provides a reasonable safety
margin.

Source: Xiao W, Yang H, Hao Z, et al. Relationship between fear-avoidance beliefs and reaction time changes prior to
and following exercise-induced muscle fatigue in chronic low back pain. Pain Res Manag. 2024;2024:9982411.
doi:10.1155/2024/9982411.
